Colored Noise

Colored Noise, put simply, if White Noise with a skewed random probability function. Higher or lower values might be more likely to occur. Higher or lower values might not be completely filtered out. So long as the frequency distribution of wavelengths is uneven, the noise is considered colored.

In this page, I'll represent a few of the most common types of colors noises can take on. Keep in mind that, just like White Noise, each of these color methods can be used to generate both 1D sounds and 2D textures.
